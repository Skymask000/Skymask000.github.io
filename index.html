Skymask browser extensions

Small, free, open-source extensions for YouTube. No accounts, no analytics, no tracking.

YT Playlist Builder

Paste a list of songs, get a YouTube playlist. Finds a video for each entry, shows you every match before anything is added, and builds the playlist on your own account.

Home Privacy policy Chrome Web Store Source

YouTube Auto-Generated CC

Turns on YouTube's auto-generated captions automatically and picks the caption language by rules you set. Works entirely offline, with no account and no network requests of its own.

Chrome Web Store Source

YT Playlist Search

Search across your own YouTube playlists, or any public playlist by URL, and jump straight to the video.
